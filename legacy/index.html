📌 FMGE at a Glance

What is FMGE?

Foreign Medical Graduate Examination — mandatory licensure exam by NMC (formerly MCI) for Indian students who studied MBBS abroad. Conducted twice a year (June & December).

Pattern

  • 300 MCQs total
  • 2 papers × 150 Qs (2.5 hrs each)
  • No negative marking
  • Pass = 50% (150 marks)

Pass Rate Reality

Historical pass rate: 15–25%. Low pass rate is mostly due to under-preparation, not exam difficulty. A structured 12+ month plan dramatically improves odds.
